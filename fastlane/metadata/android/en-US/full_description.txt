Titan is a workout log that runs entirely on your phone. Log every set in a tap, follow a next target that the app works out from your history, and watch your lifts add up. No account, no cloud, no ads.

LOG A WORKOUT
• Start from a template or from nothing, and tick off sets as you go: weight, reps and RPE, or time and distance for holds and cardio.
• A rest timer that keeps counting in the background and tells you when to go again.
• A plate calculator that loads the bar from the plates you actually own.
• 17 ready-made templates on day one — push, pull, legs, upper and lower body, full body, HIIT, rowing, stretching and more. Edit them, rename them, or write your own.

KNOW WHAT TO LIFT NEXT
• A next target on every set: hold the weight and add a rep, or add weight when you have earned it, matched to your goal and your experience.
• A warm-up and a cool-down for the muscles the session trains. Nothing reaches your log until you accept it.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Personal records, an estimated one-rep max and a progression chart for every exercise.
• Volume, sessions per week and time trained over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, a year or all time.
• A calendar of past sessions, and body weight and measurements over time.
• Levels, quests and achievements that reward showing up.

A LIBRARY OF MORE THAN 800 EXERCISES
Searchable, with the muscles each one works and how to perform it. Add your own.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Titan has no internet permission: nothing can leave the phone, and there is no analytics, crash reporting or advertising inside.
• Back up everything to a single file and restore it on any device, or let Titan write a copy once a week.
• Delete everything from Settings whenever you like.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Light or dark, metric or imperial.
• In English, French, Spanish and Portuguese.
• Built and checked against TalkBack, large fonts and one-handed use.
• Workout reminders on the days and at the time you choose.

Titan is free and open source under the GNU GPL v3.0. The exercise library comes from the free-exercise-db project.
